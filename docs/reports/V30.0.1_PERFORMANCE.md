# v30.0.1 Steady — Startup and Lifecycle Measurements

## Startup comparison

The comparison uses the local v29.0.1 baseline at commit
`59c6e008fd809b932dccb23e77e7912d1208b3bf` and the v30.0.1 candidate after
the workflow-page lazy-loading change. Both revisions were measured from
checkouts on the same filesystem. The order alternated between revisions for
each pair; each measurement used two warmups and one recorded cold process.
There were ten recorded measurements per revision. The benchmark used a clean
temporary profile, `QT_QPA_PLATFORM=offscreen`, and the existing
`scripts/benchmark_startup.py` harness. The completion marker was the Atlas
Dashboard becoming realized.

Home reached its marker **52.01% sooner** by median. Median RSS decreased
**5.52%**. This meets the plan's performance gate: at least a 10% improvement
in one metric and no regression above 15% in the other. The candidate also
imports 20 fewer Python modules before Home is ready and creates 234 fewer Qt
widgets during startup (307 vs. 327 imports; 321 vs. 555 widgets).

The first unpaired before/after measurements varied substantially with host
load. The alternating series above is the qualification dataset; the initial
single-order results are not used to claim the improvement.

## Worker and page lifecycle

The focused real-Qt tests ran 30 consecutive worker start/finish cycles and
asserted that every QThread was released and that the adapter's owned QObject
count remained constant at one until the adapter itself was deleted, then
returned to zero. A separate 30-cycle route test entered and left the workflow
pages and verified that lazy pages were instantiated once and reused. Both
checks are included in `tests/test_operation_controller_v30.py` and
`tests/test_v29_shell.py`.

These are offscreen local measurements. Physical Fedora desktop, Polkit,
Atomic deployment, reboot, keyboard, and Orca qualification remain
`unverified`.
